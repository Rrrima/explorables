# Thinking Inside the Box: outline

**Question (user):** does LLMs make human think worse?

**Spine.** One gap and one number. In a Turkish high school, students who practised with ChatGPT scored **48% better** on practice problems and **17% worse** on the exam that followed, once the tool was taken away [C6]. Junior developers who learned a new library with an AI assistant finished no faster and scored **17 points lower** on the quiz [C21]. The essay keeps returning to that gap: between the work the tool produces and the thinking the person keeps. The reference image is the gap drawn: a person whose head is inside the machine's thought.

- [x] How many people this concerns → ch 1 last step (900 M a week; 95% of UK students)
