# Thinking Inside the Box — explainer script

About 85 seconds, ten scenes, 220 words. Drawn live in the essay's hand: a thin grey pen, one blue watercolour, small people, a translucent cube that stands for the machine's thought.

## Talking points

1. The spine: a thousand Turkish students; with ChatGPT practice scores rose 48%, the exam fell 17% (Bastani et al. 2025, PNAS; C6).
2. The worry is 2,400 years old: Thamus on writing (Phaedrus; H1). Calculators were tested and cleared (79 studies; H11). Autopilot was tested and the FAA agreed skills fade (H24).
3. The MIT brain study: 0 of 18 ChatGPT writers could quote their own essay; 54 people, not reviewed; the authors asked journalists not to say "dumber" (C1, C2, C3).
4. The mechanism: re-reading wins at five minutes (83% vs 71%) and loses at a week (40% vs 61%) (Roediger & Karpicke 2006; M12).
5. The mirror: METR's developers were 19% slower with AI and believed they were 20% faster (C18).
6. Sameness: stories written with AI ideas were a little better individually and more alike collectively (Doshi & Hauser 2024; C14).
7. The fix: the same model told to hint, not answer: practice +127%, the exam penalty gone (C6).
8. The landing: not dumber; worse at what you let it do for you, and unable to feel it. "If it disappeared tomorrow, you would still know how to think" (H49).

## Audio plan

Voice at speed 0.92 with 0.7–1.2 s pauses between scenes. Music: quiet felt piano and a soft string pad, slow, no vocals. Effects: a soft watercolour drop, a paper whoosh, a paper rustle, a water drip, a small ding, a two-note chime at the end.
