# Sit, Stay, Think: outline

**Question (user):** how do we determine the IQ and EQ of dogs and cats, and what is the ranking over the breeds?

**Spine.** One ranking and one number. In 1994, 199 obedience judges ranked dog breeds from the Border Collie (1st) to the Afghan Hound (79th rank) [D1]. In 2022, a study of 18,385 dogs found that breed explains about **9%** of how any one dog behaves [D7]. The essay walks from the ranking to that 9%: what the tests measure, what the ranking measures, what "EQ" means for a dog or a cat, and why cats barely appear in the league table.
