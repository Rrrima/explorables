# The Half Shell — outline

**Spine:** one platter of oysters on a sunlit table. An oyster is a little jar of the water it grew in: it matches the sea's saltiness by filling its cells with amino acids (taurine ≈ 80% of them, T2), so place, season and time all end up on the tongue, and on the bill. Each chapter takes something off that platter.

Prompt checklist (every item → chapter):
- [x] something about oysters (what they are / biology) → ch3
- [x] why people like them → ch1 (history/status), ch3 (umami, sweet, salt: the taste), coda
- [x] why happy hour is associated with them → ch2
- [x] how they taste different in different places → ch4 (map) + ch5 (season)
- [x] the most expensive ones → ch6
- [x] how to tell a good oyster → ch7
Reader's next questions: is it safe (Vibrio, ch7), R-month rule (ch5), why so cheap at happy hour but $3+ otherwise (ch2), what do French numbers mean (ch6/7).
